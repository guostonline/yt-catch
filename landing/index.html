The queue

Start it. Walk away. It's done.

Drop in a single video or a 200-part playlist. Catch fetches the metadata, downloads in parallel, merges with ffmpeg and keeps you posted the whole way.

  • Live progress, speed and ETA — streamed, not polled.
  • Cancel mid-download, or clear the finished jobs.
  • Reveal any file in Finder, Explorer or your file manager.
  • Skip what you already have with the download archive.

Local by design

Your links aren't our business.

Catch runs on your machine and talks to 127.0.0.1. There's no account, no analytics endpoint, no queue in someone else's cloud. The source is right there if you don't believe us.

  • No sign-up, no telemetry, no tracking pixels.
  • Cookies are read from your browser and stay on the machine.
  • Files land in downloads/ — never in a cloud bucket.
  • Python + FastAPI backend, vanilla JS frontend, no build step.
